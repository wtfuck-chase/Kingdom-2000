You're on the waiting list

A transfer officer will reply with an alliance placement and a pass-cost estimate. We work the queue daily while a window is open, so expect an answer within 24 hours — faster if you left a Discord handle.

  1. Join the DiscordIt is where placement replies land and where the window opening is announced. Ask for the transfer channel. Click here to join our Discord!
  2. Bank your passesCost is 1 to 50 depending on your Transfer Score against our kingdom rating. The Alliance Shop gives one pass a week for around 150,000 alliance coins, so start now if you are short.
  3. Check your Town Center levelThe required level scales with kingdom age and shows on the in-game transfer screen once you select Kingdom 2000. It is the most common thing that blocks people at the gate.
  4. Empty your queues and spend downResources above Storehouse protection are lost in the move. Finish any event you have unsettled rewards in.
  5. Leave your alliance on the dayNot before — you will lose help and territory bonuses for nothing. Just before you transfer is soon enough.
  6. Land and tag up immediatelyAn untagged city here is free-for-all under clause 3 of the NAP. Your placement alliance will have an invite waiting.
