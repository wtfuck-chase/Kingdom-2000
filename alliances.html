Alliance directory

Seven alliances, four hiring, 62 seats open before the September window. Each entry carries the two things that decide whether an alliance is right for you: its Bear Trap slots, and how many accounts sit above yours.

Read the top-10 floor before the total power

Total power tells you how hard an alliance rallies. The top-10 power floor tells you whether you will ever finish in one. If your power is above an alliance's floor you are competing for the best reward tier every week; if it is well below, you are collecting participation rewards no matter how active you are. The placement estimator works this out for you.

Entry is by invitation: the alliance recruits for hero depth and rally-join discipline rather than raw score. The heaviest concentration of big spenders in the kingdom sits here, which is exactly why it wins castles and exactly why breaking into its top ten is hard.

How placement actually works

Your application goes to the transfer officer, not to an alliance R5. They match your power against each alliance's floor, your timezone against its Bear slots and shift cover, and any group or farm accounts you named. You get a placement and a reason before the window opens, and you can say no. Internal moves are open after 30 days, and the Discord is one server for the whole kingdom, so switching alliances does not cost you the people you talk to.
