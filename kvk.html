KvK archive

Every Kingdom of Power cycle Kingdom 2000 has played, including the byes and the two we lost. Prep and battle are scored separately, so read the two result columns rather than looking for a winner.

How the cycle works

Kingdom of Power runs on a 28-day global clock. Every kingdom's match happens in the same week.

Matchmaking

Kingdoms are paired on the power of the top 100 active governors within the same hero generation. Unequipping gear or disbanding troops does not lower it.

Saturday

Preparation

Five scoring days. Points come from spending banked materials — speedups, shards, Truegold, forge hammers. The prep winner travels.

Days 1–5

Battle

Borders open 10:00 UTC and close 22:00. Kill points, territory, and the castle. Shield before prep closes or you will be farmed overnight.

Weekend

Recovery

Field Triage, heals, and the transfer window. This is the only part of the cycle you can move kingdoms in.

What we ask of an incoming governor

Stop spending speedups, hero shards, forge hammers and Truegold roughly two weeks before prep opens. Prep scores the spending, not the owning, so anything burned early is points handed to the other kingdom. Shield before prep closes. Those two habits are the whole difference between our 2026 record and our January one.
