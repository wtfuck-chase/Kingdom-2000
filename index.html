The kingdom, in plain terms

What you are actually moving into, before anyone talks you into it.

2000 is a mid-age server that came out of its first Kingdom of Power intact and has stayed organised since. Leadership is a standing council of the seven R5s plus the king, meeting each Monday before the buff schedule is posted. Decisions are written down and published here — the NAP, the castle rotation, and the buff calendar are not verbal agreements.

We are a war server, not a farm server. Bear Hunt and Alliance Championship run every week with full turnout, and the top four alliances rally together in KvK rather than scoring separately. If you want a quiet kingdom where nobody is hit, this is the wrong address.

Most of our members sit in the Americas and Western Europe, which puts kingdom prime time between 13:00 and 04:00 UTC. Asia-Pacific governors are welcome, and 2KVG in particular is building an APAC garrison shift.

Where would you land?

Put your power in and this estimates your rank inside each alliance, from the power bands published above. Placement decides your reward tier far more than kingdom rank does: being 8th of 94 in an alliance that rallies is worth more every week than being 60th of 98 in the strongest one.

Enter your total power to see where you would sit in each alliance.

Bear Trap timings matter as much as power

Each alliance runs its own two Bear Hunt slots, and they are set to whichever hours that roster is actually awake. Missing both every week costs you more materials over a season than two Town Center levels gain you. Check the slot before you check the power — the estimator shows both.

NAP rules

Agreed by all seven R5s and enforced by the NAP council — R5 plus three R4s per alliance. Breaking these costs your alliance its protection, not just you. Full text and amendment history is on the NAP page.

  1. No hits inside the NAPTagged members of NAP alliances are protected at all times outside KvK battle phase.
  2. Scouting and single-troop marches are allowedScouts, 1-troop pokes and 69-troop marches are fine on anyone in the kingdom.
  3. No alliance tag means free-for-allUntagged cities are unprotected. Join an alliance before you unshield.
  4. Tents are always openTents are FFA regardless of tag. Do not tent inside contested territory and expect mercy.
  5. Three-hit cap outside the NAPNon-NAP targets may be hit a maximum of three times per target, per day.
  6. Retaliation is allowed and loggedCheck the battle report first. One alliance-mate may take the victim's place to answer. Report it so the council can track it.
  7. Banner and HQ attacks must be scheduledAnnounce in the kingdom channel and clear it with the defending R5 before marching.
  8. The castle rotatesHolders drop the castle before the three-hour timer so the window reopens and every alliance can collect.
  9. R5s answer for their membersIf an alliance repeatedly breaks the pact and its R5 does nothing, that R5 becomes a valid target.
  10. Sanctuaries and fortresses are negotiatedOwnership is agreed at the Monday council, not taken by surprise. Holds are shared on rotation.

Apply to transfer

The form takes two minutes. A transfer officer replies with your alliance placement and your estimated pass cost before the window opens on 13 September.

Submissions go to the transfer officers' Discord channel. We keep your governor name, kingdom, power and timezone until this window closes, then the list is deleted. No email address is asked for.

  • Town Center at or above the level shown on your in-game transfer screen
  • Left your alliance before the window opens
  • At least 25 days since your last transfer
  • Fewer than four characters already in Kingdom 2000
  • Same hero generation and Truegold stage as 2000
  • Enough Transfer Passes — 1 to 50 depending on your power against our kingdom rating
  • Not currently a King or Transfer Manager
  • Not in an active battle when you move

Pass cost is set by your Transfer Score against our kingdom rating, so we cannot quote it for you — but the confirm screen in game can, and our officers will tell you if you are over a cap before you spend anything. Resources above your Storehouse protection are lost in the move, so empty your queues first.
